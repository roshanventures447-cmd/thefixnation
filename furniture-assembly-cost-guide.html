There is no honest single price for every bed, wardrobe or cabinet. This guide explains the variables a carpenter checks and how to get a clearer quote before work starts.

Before approving work

Ask the carpenter to explain labour, drilling, dismantling, extra fitting and replacement-part charges after inspecting the product.

Quote factors

Six details that can change the final assembly charge.

01Furniture type

A study table and a hydraulic storage bed have very different assembly steps.

02Product size

Large wardrobes and multi-panel units normally require more handling and alignment.

03Parts condition

Missing screws, damaged boards or incorrect hardware can delay or change the work.

04Wall drilling

Wall-mounted cabinets and shelves may require drilling, anchors and wall-condition checks.

05Dismantling

Removing an old fitting before assembly adds a separate work step.

06Access and time

Building access, product location and difficult handling can affect visit duration.

Prepare once

Share these details for a clearer callback.

  • Product name, purchase link or model number if available.
  • One full furniture photo and close photos of parts or damage.
  • Whether the product is new, partially assembled or needs dismantling.
  • Any missing screws, boards, handles, hinges or drawer channels.
  • Complete address, building access and preferred callback time.
  • Whether wall drilling or old furniture removal may be required.

FAQ

Common furniture assembly cost questions.

Can the final quote be confirmed without seeing the furniture?

Photos and product links help with an initial understanding, but the final quote may still depend on actual parts, condition, drilling and fitting scope at the location.

Does the final charge include replacement parts?

Replacement hardware or parts should be discussed separately. Ask for a clear split between labour and material before approving additional work.

Can multiple services be booked together?

Yes. Add multiple services to the cart. The Rs 49 amount confirms the combined visit request; final work charges are quoted after inspection.
