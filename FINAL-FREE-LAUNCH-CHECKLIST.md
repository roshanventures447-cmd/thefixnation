# The Fix Nation Final Free Launch Checklist

Updated: 2026-08-09

## 1. Upload the new website files

Upload the complete latest folder to GitHub or hosting:

- `index.html`
- all `.html` pages
- `sitemap.xml`
- `robots.txt`
- `css/`
- `js/`
- `img/`

Do not upload the old folder again after this build.

## 2. Check the live site after upload

Open these URLs in the browser:

- `https://thefixnation.com/`
- `https://thefixnation.com/blogs.html`
- `https://thefixnation.com/city-service-pages.html`
- `https://thefixnation.com/sofa-installation-guide.html`
- `https://thefixnation.com/sofa-repair-guide.html`
- `https://thefixnation.com/bed-repair-guide.html`
- `https://thefixnation.com/sitemap.xml`

Confirm:

- homepage slider changes every 2 seconds
- bed installation, sofa installation, sofa repair and bed repair images load
- service category clicks show the correct service
- booking form creates an automatic reference
- worker form saves to the worker sheet
- sitemap opens without error

## 4. Free traffic routine

Daily for 30 minutes:

- Add one real customer question and answer to a guide or blog.
- Post one service photo or work update on Google Business Profile.
- Ask every completed customer for a Google review with city and service name.
- Share one service page in WhatsApp groups only where it is relevant.
- Track calls by writing city, service and source in Google Sheet.

## 5. Payment note

The current free flow can open UPI apps and save payment status as pending verification. For direct confirmed payment without customer manual reference, use Paytm or another real payment gateway with backend verification.

Never paste a Paytm Merchant Key in frontend files.
