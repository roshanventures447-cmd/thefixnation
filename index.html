Questions before booking

Common booking questions.

How do I book a furniture service?

Select city, save address, add the service to cart and confirm the visit. The team calls before assigning the professional.

Is Rs 49 the full service charge?

No. Rs 49 confirms the booking request. The final service charge is shared after the professional checks the furniture, parts and work scope.

Can I book more than one service?

Yes. Add multiple services to the cart, such as bed assembly plus wardrobe fitting or AC service plus electrician visit.

Can workers join from other cities?

Yes. Professionals can apply from all active cities. The onboarding team reviews skill, tools, area and availability before assigning work.
