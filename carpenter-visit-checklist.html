Before the visit

Carpenter visit checklist for furniture assembly and repair.

A few prepared details can reduce delays, prevent missing-part confusion and make the final work quote easier to understand.

By The Fix Nation Service Operations TeamUpdated June 13, 20266 minute read
ChecklistBefore bookingPrepare the productPrepare the locationReview the quoteBefore completion
Step 1

Give the callback team a complete requirement.

  • Select the exact service: bed, wardrobe, table, cabinet, repair or consultation.
  • Share the complete address and a working mobile number.
  • Mention whether furniture is boxed, opened, partially assembled or already installed.
  • Share a product link, invoice name or model number when available.
Step 2

Keep all boards, fittings and hardware together.

01Count packages

Check whether every box listed by the seller has arrived.

02Keep hardware safe

Place screws, hinges, handles and channels in one visible area.

03Open carefully

Avoid damaging boards or losing small parts while unpacking.

04Photograph damage

Take clear photos before assembly if a panel or fitting arrived damaged.

Step 3

Prepare a safe, accessible work area.

  • Clear enough floor space around the assembly location.
  • Confirm lift, staircase, parking or entry restrictions.
  • Keep children and pets away from tools and unpacked hardware.
  • Tell the carpenter before drilling if the wall may contain electrical or plumbing lines.
  • Take housing-society permission when drilling or service timing is restricted.
Wall-mounted furniture

Cabinets and shelves need a wall-condition check. The carpenter should confirm anchor type, load, level and safe drilling points before work.

Step 4

Approve the final quote only after scope is clear.

Ask for a clear explanation of labour, dismantling, drilling, additional fitting and replacement-part costs. If the actual furniture differs from the booking description, the quote may change.

Ask aboutWhy it matters
Labour scopeConfirms exactly which furniture and fittings are included.
Extra partsSeparates material cost from labour.
DrillingClarifies wall fixing, anchors and additional work.
Repair limitsConfirms whether damage can be repaired safely.
Step 5

Check the furniture before the professional leaves.

  • Open and close doors, drawers and storage mechanisms.
  • Check table, chair or bed stability without applying unusual force.
  • Review alignment, handles, hinges and visible gaps.
  • Collect unused hardware and keep product documents.
  • Raise any concern before confirming completion.
