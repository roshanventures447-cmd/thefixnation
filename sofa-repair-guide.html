Sofa repair guide

Sofa repair at home: how to explain the problem clearly.

Good repair starts with a clear issue photo and honest inspection. This guide helps customers understand what can be checked during a sofa repair visit.

By The Fix Nation Service Operations TeamUpdated August 9, 20266 minute read

Repair scope

Common sofa problems a carpenter can inspect.

The visit can cover loose wooden frame, damaged leg, weak support, shaky brackets, handle or hinge issue, minor panel alignment and hardware tightening. The team confirms whether the issue is carpenter-side repair or upholstery work before assignment.

Before callback

Share photos that make the repair request real.

  • One full sofa photo from the front.
  • Close photo of broken leg, loose joint or damaged frame.
  • Photo from below if the issue is support related.
  • Short note: when the issue started and what changed.
  • City, full address and preferred callback time.
Trust builder

Clear photos reduce wrong assignment and help the team tell you whether a carpenter visit is suitable.

Final quote

Why sofa repair price changes after inspection.

01Damage depth

Surface tightening and structural repair have different effort.

02Parts need

Brackets, screws, legs or support pieces may be required.

03Access

Some sofas need lifting, turning or partial dismantling.

04Repair feasibility

The professional explains if repair is possible before work starts.

Customer approval

Approve the final scope before work begins.

Ask the professional to explain labour, material and repair risk clearly. The Fix Nation process keeps booking confirmation separate from final work approval.

FAQ

Common sofa repair questions.

Can the sofa be repaired in one visit?

Minor fitting issues may close in one visit. If special parts or upholstery are needed, the team will explain next steps.

Do I need to pay before final quote?

Only the booking confirmation is paid first. Final work charge is discussed after inspection.
