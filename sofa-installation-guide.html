Sofa installation after delivery: what to check before booking.

New sofa setup looks simple, but wrong leg fitting, loose hardware or poor alignment can damage the product. This guide explains what The Fix Nation checks before confirming a visit.

By The Fix Nation Service Operations TeamUpdated August 9, 20266 minute read

Scope

What sofa installation usually includes.

The work can include sofa leg fitting, sectional sofa joining, recliner panel alignment, loose bracket tightening, handle or support fitting and basic setup after delivery. The exact scope depends on the product design and available hardware.

Important

If any part is missing, damaged or incorrectly supplied by the seller, the professional will explain the issue before starting extra work.

Preparation

Keep these items ready for a faster visit.

  • All legs, screws, brackets, bolts and fitting tools supplied in the box.
  • Product invoice, order page or model number if available.
  • Clear photos of the sofa, packed parts and problem area.
  • Enough working space around the sofa location.
  • Lift, parking and society entry details if needed.
Booking charge

Rs 49 confirms the request; final quote comes after inspection.

The Rs 49 amount confirms your booking request and allows the team to create an automatic reference. Final labour or material charge depends on sofa size, parts condition, time, tools and handling effort.
